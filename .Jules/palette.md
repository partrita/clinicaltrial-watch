## 2025-05-15 - [Enhancing categorical scannability with badges]
**Learning:** Using color-coded Bootstrap badges for clinical trial phases significantly improves scannability compared to plain text, especially when multiple phases are present. Including `aria-label` ensures this visual information is accessible to screen readers.
**Action:** Use small, distinct badges for multi-value categorical data (like phases or categories) to improve scannability, and always pair them with ARIA labels for accessibility.
